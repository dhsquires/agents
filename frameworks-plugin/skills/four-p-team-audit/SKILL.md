# People → Purpose → Process → Platform (4P)

> Diagnose a team across four dimensions and fix the lowest-scoring one first.

**Author:** Nils Vinje, Founder, Glide Consulting
**Source:** Glide Consulting's proprietary Customer Success framework; applied across VP-level CS organizations
**Field-tested:** Applied in enterprise SaaS Customer Success organizations; underpins Glide Consulting's engagements

## When to use this
- Auditing the health of a team or function
- Diagnosing why a team is underperforming or not scaling
- Onboarding as a new leader and assessing what to fix first
- Prioritizing where to invest limited improvement effort
- Reviewing a Customer Success or other operational org

## The framework
- **People** — Do you have the right talent in the right roles with the right competencies?
- **Purpose** — Is there a clear, shared understanding of why the team exists and what success looks like?
- **Process** — Are workflows documented, repeatable, and scalable?
- **Platform** — Do the tools support the process, or is the team working around them?

## How to run it (step by step)
Audit each dimension using 1–10 scoring:
1. People: Are roles clearly defined? Do competencies match responsibilities?
2. Purpose: Can every team member articulate the mission in one sentence?
3. Process: Which processes are ad hoc vs. documented?
4. Platform: Where does tooling create friction instead of removing it?

## Facilitating with the user
When this skill activates, you (Claude) should:
1. Restate or gather the user's context (team size, function, current challenges)
2. Score each of the four dimensions 1–10 with specific rationale
3. Make any filled-in gaps explicit and labeled as assumptions
4. Flag the step where the analysis is weakest or you lack data
5. Suggest 1-2 complementary frameworks for a second pass

## Output template
- **People — score /10:** rationale
- **Purpose — score /10:** rationale
- **Process — score /10:** rationale
- **Platform — score /10:** rationale
- **Lowest-scoring dimension:** the bottleneck
- **90-day improvement plan:** concrete actions for the weakest dimension

## Watch out for
- Scoring on gut feel without evidence for each dimension
- Fixing tooling (Platform) when the real gap is Purpose or People
- Treating the four dimensions as independent — they reinforce each other
- Confusing busy, documented Process with one that actually scales
