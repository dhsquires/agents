# LLM Brain Interview Protocol

This is the script for populating the [brain/](../brain/index.md) bundle. The goal is a rich, honest, first-person portrait — not a survey. Run it as a conversation, not a form.

## Methodology

- **One question at a time.** Ask a single question, wait for the full answer, then decide what to ask next. Never batch questions — it produces shallow, list-shaped replies.
- **Active probing.** Treat the first answer as the headline, not the story. Follow up until you reach something specific and lived. Each primary question below ships with PROBES for exactly this.
- **Surface → depth progression.** Open a module with an easy, concrete question; move toward meaning and emotion only after rapport and momentum exist within the module.
- **Behavioral and narrative over abstract.** Prefer "Tell me about a time…" and "Walk me through…" over "What do you value?" People reveal themselves in stories and specifics; abstractions invite slogans.
- **Probe triggers — signals of a shallow answer.** Dig deeper when you hear: generic virtues ("hard work", "honesty") with no example; textbook or résumé phrasing; one-word or list answers with no story; hedging or visible avoidance; an answer that could be true of anyone. Each question notes its specific trigger.
- **Capture, don't editorialize.** Record the person's own words and framing into the target file. Do not invent facts. Mark anything inferred as inferred.
- **Respect sensitivity.** Modules 7-9 (relationships, health, finances) are personal. Let the person set the depth; never push past a clear boundary. Store no credentials or account numbers.

How to use each module: ask the primary questions in order, branch into PROBES as warranted, and write the results into the mapped file's sections. Then move on.

## Module 1 — Core Identity → [brain/core/identity.md](../brain/core/identity.md)

**1.1 How would you describe yourself to someone who'll never meet you?**
- PROBE: Which of those did you list first, and why that one?
- PROBE: What did you leave out that's actually important?
- PROBE: Which part would surprise people who know you casually?
- Probe trigger: answer reads like a bio or job title — push for the person behind the role.

**1.2 When do you feel most like yourself?**
- PROBE: What's happening around you in that moment?
- PROBE: When do you feel least like yourself?
- Probe trigger: vague ("when I'm relaxed") — ask for a concrete recent instance.

**1.3 What's a label people apply to you that feels wrong?**
- PROBE: Where do you think that misread comes from?
- PROBE: What would be more accurate?
- Probe trigger: they can't name one — try the inverse (a label they wish stuck).

**1.4 What drives you when no one is watching?**
- PROBE: Has that engine always been there, or did it change?
- PROBE: What happens to you when it's blocked?
- Probe trigger: a virtuous-sounding motive with no behavioral evidence — ask for the last time it actually moved them.

## Module 2 — Values → [brain/core/values.md](../brain/core/values.md)

**2.1 Tell me about a time you walked away from something on principle.**
- PROBE: What did it cost you?
- PROBE: Would you do it again the same way?
- Probe trigger: hypothetical instead of a real event — ask for something that actually happened.

**2.2 Where do your stated values and your calendar/spending disagree?**
- PROBE: Why do you think that gap exists?
- PROBE: Does it bother you, or have you made peace with it?
- Probe trigger: claims perfect alignment — gently press; near everyone has a gap.

**2.3 What's a line you wouldn't cross even for a lot of money or status?**
- PROBE: Has that line ever been tested?
- PROBE: Is there a price or pressure that would move it?
- Probe trigger: generic morality — anchor to a concrete scenario in their actual life.

**2.4 When two things you care about collide, which usually wins?**
- PROBE: Give me an example of that collision.
- PROBE: How did you feel about the resolution afterward?
- Probe trigger: "it depends" with no example — supply a realistic tension and ask.

---

## Module 3 — Goals → [brain/core/goals.md](../brain/core/goals.md)

**3.1 What are you actually working toward right now?**
- PROBE: What does "done" look like for that?
- PROBE: Why this, why now?
- Probe trigger: aspirational vagueness ("be successful") — push for an observable outcome.

**3.2 Picture a good life 10-15 years out. What's in the frame?**
- PROBE: What's deliberately *not* in the frame?
- PROBE: What would have to be true to get there?
- Probe trigger: a stock image of success — ask what *they* specifically want in it.

**3.3 What outcome would make you feel you'd wasted the decade?**
- PROBE: How close or far are you from that right now?
- PROBE: What guards against it?
- Probe trigger: can't name an anti-goal — reframe as "what would count as failure?"

**3.4 If you had to drop one current goal tomorrow, which goes first?**
- PROBE: What does that ranking tell you?
- Probe trigger: refuses to choose — note the avoidance and ask what makes ranking hard.

## Module 4 — Career → [brain/professional/career.md](../brain/professional/career.md)

**4.1 Walk me through a real day from last week.**
- PROBE: How much of that is what you're actually paid/valued for?
- PROBE: Which parts energized you, which drained you?
- Probe trigger: describes the job description, not the day — ask for specific hours.

**4.2 How did you end up doing this? Trace the path.**
- PROBE: Which turns were chosen vs. accidental?
- PROBE: Is the path still pointing where you want?
- Probe trigger: a tidy linear story — probe for the messy or accidental parts.

**4.3 What can you do that few people around you can?**
- PROBE: How do you know — what's the evidence?
- PROBE: Where are you only *competent*, not deep?
- Probe trigger: modesty or bravado — anchor to concrete tasks and outcomes.

**4.4 What work are you genuinely proud of, and why that one?**
- PROBE: What was hard about it?
- Probe trigger: lists a title/promotion — ask for the work itself and its impact.

## Module 5 — Work Style → [brain/professional/work-style.md](../brain/professional/work-style.md)

**5.1 Think of your best work — what were the conditions?**
- PROBE: Time of day, environment, alone or with others?
- PROBE: What reliably ruins those conditions?
- Probe trigger: "I can work anywhere" — push for the actual best case.

**5.2 How do you make decisions — fast or slow, gut or data?**
- PROBE: Give me a recent decision and how you made it.
- PROBE: Does it differ for reversible vs. irreversible calls?
- Probe trigger: idealized ("carefully and rationally") — ask for a real recent example.

**5.3 How do you want to be disagreed with?**
- PROBE: When has disagreement landed well vs. badly?
- Probe trigger: "I'm totally open" — test with how they reacted to recent pushback.

**5.4 What does an AI assistant do that makes you stop using it?**
- PROBE: And what makes one worth keeping?
- PROBE: How much should it push back vs. just do the task?
- Probe trigger: vague — get concrete enough to become Always/Never rules.

## Module 6 — Learning → [brain/professional/learning.md](../brain/professional/learning.md)

**6.1 Pick something you got genuinely good at. How did that actually happen?**
- PROBE: Reading, doing, teaching, watching — what was the real mechanism?
- PROBE: Depth-first or breadth-first?
- Probe trigger: "I just practiced" — unpack the actual loop.

**6.2 What are you trying to learn right now, and how's it going?**
- PROBE: What's the motivation underneath it?
- PROBE: What's slowing you down?
- Probe trigger: aspirational-only ("I want to learn X") with no action — note the gap.

**6.3 What's in your input stream in a normal week?**
- PROBE: How do you decide what's worth your attention?
- PROBE: What have you cut out deliberately?
- Probe trigger: name-drops without engagement — ask what they actually finished.

---

## Module 7 — Relationships → [brain/personal/relationships.md](../brain/personal/relationships.md)

> _Sensitive. Let them set the depth._

**7.1 Who do you call when something goes wrong?**
- PROBE: What role does that person play for you?
- PROBE: Who calls *you*?
- Probe trigger: deflects to "lots of people" — ask for the specific first call.

**7.2 What pattern shows up across your closest relationships?**
- PROBE: Is it one you like?
- PROBE: Where might it come from?
- Probe trigger: can't see a pattern — offer to explore via two specific relationships.

**7.3 How do you handle conflict with someone you love?**
- PROBE: Walk me through a recent example.
- PROBE: How do you repair afterward?
- Probe trigger: "I avoid conflict" / "we don't fight" — gently probe what happens to the friction.

**7.4 How much and what kind of connection do you actually need?**
- PROBE: How do you recharge socially?
- Probe trigger: a "should" answer — separate what they want from what they think is expected.

---

## Module 8 — Health → [brain/personal/health.md](../brain/personal/health.md)

> _Sensitive. Record only what they volunteer._

**8.1 When in the day are you sharpest, and when do you fade?**
- PROBE: How does your week's rhythm work?
- PROBE: What restores you vs. what just feels restful?
- Probe trigger: "I'm a morning person" with no detail — get the actual curve.

**8.2 What's the first sign you're overloaded?**
- PROBE: What tends to trigger it?
- PROBE: What actually helps — not what you're supposed to do?
- Probe trigger: textbook self-care answers — ask what genuinely works for *them*.

**8.3 How would you describe your physical and mental baseline right now?**
- PROBE: Anything ongoing that shapes daily life?
- PROBE: What support is in place?
- Probe trigger: clearly uncomfortable — back off and capture only what's offered.

---

## Module 9 — Finances → [brain/personal/finances.md](../brain/personal/finances.md)

> _Sensitive. Values over figures. Store no credentials/account numbers._

**9.1 What does money buy you that matters most — security, freedom, status, options?**
- PROBE: Where does that come from?
- PROBE: Has the answer changed over time?
- Probe trigger: "I don't really care about money" — probe behavior, which usually says otherwise.

**9.2 What do you spend on without guilt, and what makes you wince?**
- PROBE: What does that split reveal?
- Probe trigger: generic — anchor to a recent purchase they felt strongly about.

**9.3 What financial outcome are you working toward?**
- PROBE: What's the time horizon?
- PROBE: What do you worry about financially?
- Probe trigger: no concrete target — explore whether avoidance or contentment.

---

## Module 10 — Lifestyle → [brain/personal/lifestyle.md](../brain/personal/lifestyle.md)

**10.1 Walk me through an ordinary Saturday.**
- PROBE: How close is that to your ideal one?
- PROBE: What anchors your week?
- Probe trigger: "nothing special" — the texture is the point; ask for the actual sequence.

**10.2 What do you do purely because you enjoy it, no other point?**
- PROBE: How deep does it go?
- PROBE: Solo or with others?
- Probe trigger: can't name one — explore what happened to play in their life.

**10.3 Tell me about where you live and how it suits you.**
- PROBE: What would you change tomorrow if you could?
- Probe trigger: purely factual — ask how the environment makes them feel.

**10.4 What are you into right now — watching, reading, listening?**
- PROBE: What does your taste say about you?
- Probe trigger: list with no engagement — ask what they actually loved recently.

---

## Module 11 — Worldview / Philosophy → [brain/worldview/philosophy.md](../brain/worldview/philosophy.md)

**11.1 Is there a model or framework you find yourself applying to everything?**
- PROBE: Give me a time you used it recently.
- PROBE: Where does it break down?
- Probe trigger: names a famous concept generically — ask how *they* actually use it.

**11.2 How do you decide whether to believe something?**
- PROBE: Who or what do you trust as a source?
- PROBE: What's your tolerance for "I don't know"?
- Probe trigger: "I look at the evidence" — push on how they handle conflicting evidence.

**11.3 Tell me about a belief you changed your mind on. What moved you?**
- PROBE: How long did it take?
- PROBE: Was it data, a person, or an experience?
- Probe trigger: can't recall one — that itself is signal; explore openness to updating.

**11.4 What, if anything, do you treat as sacred or non-negotiable about how the world works?**
- PROBE: Where did that conviction form?
- Probe trigger: deflects — try meaning, agency, or human nature as entry points.

---

## Module 12 — Life Narrative → [brain/worldview/narrative.md](../brain/worldview/narrative.md)

**12.1 If your life were a book, what are the chapter titles?**
- PROBE: Which chapter were you most yourself in?
- PROBE: What chapter are you in now?
- Probe trigger: gives a flat chronology — ask which chapters carry the emotional weight.

**12.2 What experience changed your trajectory the most?**
- PROBE: What would be different if it hadn't happened?
- PROBE: How do you feel about it now vs. then?
- Probe trigger: picks an obvious milestone — ask whether a quieter moment mattered more.

**12.3 Looking back, what's the thread running through all of it?**
- PROBE: Is that a thread you chose or one you noticed?
- Probe trigger: can't find one — offer to reflect the recurring themes you heard across modules.

**12.4 What chapter is still unwritten — what's unresolved?**
- PROBE: What would resolving it look like?
- Probe trigger: deflection — note it gently; unresolved is allowed to stay unresolved.

## After the interview

1. **Generate the 300-word identity synthesis.** Using the answers from all modules — especially Module 1 — write a tight, first-person, ~300-word synthesis of who this person is, and place it in the **Synthesis** section of [brain/core/identity.md](../brain/core/identity.md). Keep it honest and specific; no filler.
2. **Backfill each target file** with the captured content, in the person's own words and framing. Add `# Citations` sections where claims trace to external sources, and materialize those sources under [brain/references/index.md](../brain/references/index.md).
3. **Refresh [brain/boot-context.md](../brain/boot-context.md)** with the new identity snapshot and current focus.
4. **Update [brain/log.md](../brain/log.md)** with a new dated entry (newest-first) describing what was populated in this session.
